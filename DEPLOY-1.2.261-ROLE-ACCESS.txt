Rose Gardens v1.2.263 - Role Access deployment

From the active Firebase project root (the project whose functions source is functions/index.js):

1) Deploy the two Role Access callables:
   export FUNCTIONS_DISCOVERY_TIMEOUT=60
   firebase deploy --only functions:getRoleAccessMatrix,functions:saveRoleAccessRole

2) Deploy Firestore rules:
   firebase deploy --only firestore:rules

3) Deploy Hosting:
   firebase deploy --only hosting

After deployment, sign out completely, sign in as Admin, open Role Access, edit a non-Admin role and press Save / Update. The success message must read that Role Access was saved and verified in Firebase.
