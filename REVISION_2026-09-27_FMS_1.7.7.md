# FMS 1.7.7 - DISHA PoM Pending Days Sort

- DISHA home register sorts by PoM Pending Days in descending order.
- Full-screen/filtered DISHA registers use the same descending PoM Pending Days order.
- Records with PoM already uploaded or without applicable pending days are placed after pending records.
- Dynamic Sl.No. is regenerated from the displayed sorted order.
- Financial Year filters and prior DISHA behavior are retained.
