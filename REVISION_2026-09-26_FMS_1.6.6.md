# FMS v1.6.6 — Common Grievance Workspace

- All grievance types except LAQ and LCQ now use the CPGRAMS dashboard card set.
- All grievance types except LAQ and LCQ now use the CPGRAMS register columns and common data-entry form layout/processing.

- Dashboard card drill-down continues to open the filtered full-screen register.
