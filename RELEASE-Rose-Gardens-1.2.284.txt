Rose Gardens v1.2.284
Date: 2026-09-13

Resident Login Eligibility
- Resident Type = Owner: included for every Occupation Status.
- Resident Type = Tenant: included for every Occupation Status.
- Resident Type = Family Member: included for every Occupation Status.
- Other Resident Types remain excluded.
- Login directory remains alphabetically sorted by resident name.
- Firebase Cloud Functions residentLoginDirectory, residentLoginPhone and activateResidentSession use the same eligibility rule.

Resident Dashboard
- Owner, Tenant and Family Member resident sessions can open the Resident Dashboard.
- Tenant/Family Member sessions do not inherit Colony Fund payment history from another resident sharing the same H.No.

Colony Fund
- Eligibility remains Resident Type = Owner only.
- All Occupation Status values are included for Owners.
- Tenant and Family Member records remain excluded from Colony Fund Expected, Collected and Pending calculations.
- Tenant/Family Member Resident Portal sessions show Colony Fund as not applicable and cannot initiate Colony Fund payment from their resident record.

Version
- Web/cache version 1.2.284.
- Android versionCode 284 / versionName 1.2.284.
