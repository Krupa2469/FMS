Rose Gardens Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 August 2026

Rose Gardens Residents Welfare Association uses this application to provide resident welfare, association administration, communications, payments and public-service information.

Information used

The app may process resident name, house number reference, registered mobile number, officer account information, association records and information voluntarily submitted through complaints or service requests. Location may be requested only for live weather, air-quality and nearby-services features.

Authentication

Resident authentication uses Firebase Phone Authentication. Office-bearer authentication uses Firebase Email/Password. Authentication information is used to verify access to the appropriate dashboard.

Data sharing

Association data is used for association administration. Firebase services and approved backend providers may process data as necessary to provide authentication, storage and application functions. The association does not sell resident personal information.

Security

Access is controlled by authenticated user roles and dashboard assignments. Administrative access is restricted to authorized association administrators.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the Rose Gardens Residents Welfare Association through its designated administrator.
