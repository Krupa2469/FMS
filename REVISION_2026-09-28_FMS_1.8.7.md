# FMS 1.8.7 — DISHA Selected-FY District Cards

- DISHA “Districts with no meetings” now displays only the selected Financial Year value.
- DISHA “Districts conducted meetings” now displays only the selected Financial Year value.
- Removed all-years Total from these two district cards only.
- Meetings Held retains Total + selected FY.
- District calculations remain based on the 33-district master and unique districts with a meeting date in the selected FY.
