RGMS 1.2.247 - Fix 30

Sponsor and cash contribution are now independent Ganesh Festival attributes.

Changes:
- A Sponsor may also contribute a cash amount.
- Sponsor cash is included in Ganesh Contributions, totals, dashboards, reports and daily ledger when applicable.
- The same resident remains listed in the Sponsors section/count.
- Historical Sponsor records with an existing positive cash/amount field are treated as paid cash contributions when no explicit payment status exists.
- Sponsor data entry now includes optional Cash Contribution Amount, Payment Status and Date of Payment.
- Sponsor registers/reports show the cash contribution separately.
- Sponsor status never excludes a valid cash contribution from the Contributions report.
- Raju Naik Banoth can therefore appear both as a Sponsor and as a Contributor.
