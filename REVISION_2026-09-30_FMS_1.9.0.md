# FMS v1.9.0 — 30/09/2026

## DISHA Meetings Status Report
- Replaced the **Meetings Held** summary card with **Districts Conducted Meetings**.
- The new card counts unique districts among meetings marked Held in the selected report period / financial year.

## CPGRAMS Appeals Dashboard
- Added **Appeals Received**, **Appeals Pending**, and **Appeals Disposed** dashboard cards for CPGRAMS.
- Each appeal card opens the CPGRAMS register filtered to the corresponding appeal status and selected financial year.

## RTI Appeals Dashboard
- Added First Appeal cards: **Received**, **Pending**, **Disposed**.
- Added Second Appeal cards: **Received**, **Pending**, **Disposed**.
- Each card opens the RTI register filtered to the corresponding appeal stage and selected financial year.

## Compatibility
- Retains v1.8.9 CPGRAMS and RTI appeal fields and existing DISHA, CRUD, date, export, and Firestore behavior.
