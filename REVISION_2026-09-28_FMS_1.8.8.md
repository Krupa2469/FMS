# FMS 1.8.8

- DISHA PoM pending duration now displays calendar years/months/days, omitting zero years and zero months.
- For pending PoM after a past meeting, Remarks shows: PoM pending for N days.
- For a future meeting, Remarks shows: N days left for the meeting to be held.
- For a meeting scheduled today, Remarks shows: Meeting is scheduled for today.
- Uploaded PoM records retain their saved Remarks.
- Existing FY dashboard, calendar, CRUD foreground, form clear and auto-return behavior retained.
