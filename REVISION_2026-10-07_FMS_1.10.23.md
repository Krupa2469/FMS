# FMS Version 1.10.23 — 07-Oct-2026

## Appeals summary cards in Reports and Reports Master

Added CPGRAMS appeal dashboard metrics to the GRIEVANCES report summary configuration.

### New Summary Cards
- Appeals Received
- Appeals Pending
- Appeals Disposed

### Behaviour
- The three cards are available for selection in Reports Master under the GRIEVANCES module.
- When selected, they appear in Report Summary and Export Preview.
- Counts are calculated from the complete selected Financial Year / date-range CPGRAMS dataset, not from section-filtered rows.
- Appeals Pending excludes appeals that are disposed/closed/completed or have an Appeal Disposal Date.
- Existing report sections, section-wise filters, custom headings, sorting, exports, print, share and other summary cards remain unchanged.
