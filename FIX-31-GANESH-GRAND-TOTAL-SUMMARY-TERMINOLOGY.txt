RGMS 1.2.250 - Fix 31

Ganesh Dashboard and Reports terminology / calculation presentation:
- Total Festival Fund / Total Amount -> Grand Total (Laddu + Contributions)
- Total Amount Paid -> Contributions Received
- Total Advance Paid -> Amount Spent
- Balance Amount -> Balance Amount (Grand Total - Amount Spent)

Calculation basis remains authoritative and unchanged:
- Contributions Received = paid Ganesh cash contributions (including sponsor cash contributions when paid).
- Laddu Amount = paid Laddu Auction collections.
- Grand Total = Contributions Received + Laddu Amount.
- Amount Spent = actual expenditure amount paid/advanced.
- Balance Amount = Grand Total - Amount Spent.

The same wording is applied to the on-screen Ganesh summary, report main amount card, configured report summaries, filtered balance drill-down, Ganesh expenditure summary, and JPEG report summary.
