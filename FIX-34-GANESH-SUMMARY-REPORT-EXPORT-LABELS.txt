Rose Gardens Web 1.2.251 - Fix 34

Ganesh Dashboard, report summaries, report drill-down registers and exports now use the approved eight figures:
1. Expected Contributions = Total Contributions Paid + Contributions Due
2. Total Contributions Paid = contribution amounts with Payment Status Paid
3. Contributions Due = contribution amounts with Payment Status To be paid
4. Laddu Auction Amount = paid Laddu Auction collections
5. Total Festival Fund = Total Contributions Paid + Laddu Auction Amount
6. Expenditure = Ganesh Festival expenditure actually paid/advanced
7. Balance = Total Festival Fund - Expenditure
8. Sponsors = current sponsor count

PDF, Excel and JPEG exports use the same labels/calculations as the on-screen report.
