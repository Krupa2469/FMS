Rose Gardens Web 1.2.252 - Fix 35

Ganesh Festival dashboard, Reports dashboard, filtered registers and exports now use:
1. Expected Contributions
2. Total Contributions Paid
3. Contributions Due
4. Laddu Auction Amount
5. Total Festival Fund
6. Projected Expenditure = total amount committed for Ganesh Festival expenditure
7. Expenditure Incurred = amount actually paid / advanced against Ganesh Festival expenditure
8. Amount Required = Projected Expenditure - Expenditure Incurred
9. Balance = Total Festival Fund - Expenditure Incurred
10. Sponsors

The Ganesh expenditure section in report registers/exports uses the columns Projected Expenditure, Expenditure Incurred and Amount Required. PDF, Excel and JPEG outputs use the same terminology and calculations.
